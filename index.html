How to Install in Gmail / Outlook
1

Click "Copy Signature" on whichever card vibe or layout you prefer.

2

Open Gmail Settings → General → Signature (or Outlook Settings).

3

Paste (Ctrl + V / Cmd + V) into the signature box.

4

Scroll down and hit Save Changes. Your new signature is live!
